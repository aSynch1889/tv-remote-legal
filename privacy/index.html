Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how TV Remote - Universal Control (“we”, “us”, or “the App”) handles information when you use our native iOS application.

生效日期：2026 年 8 月 13 日 · 最近更新：2026 年 8 月 13 日

隐私政策

本隐私政策说明 TV Remote - Universal Control（“我们”或“本应用”）在你使用这款原生 iOS 应用时如何处理信息。

1. Summary

  • The App is a local Wi‑Fi remote. It discovers and controls compatible TVs on your home network.
  • No account, registration, or profile is required.
  • We do not operate a user backend that receives your TV IP addresses, pairing tokens, or browsing history.
  • In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment card details.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs that track you across apps or websites.

2. Information processed on your device

A. Local network data

To find and control smart TVs (for example Roku, Samsung, and experimental LG support), the App uses Apple networking APIs to perform local discovery such as SSDP and Bonjour/mDNS on the same Wi‑Fi network.

  • Local IP addresses, device names, and connection endpoints of discovered TVs may be stored on your iPhone so you can reconnect.
  • We do not upload local IP addresses, Wi‑Fi names, or your home network map to our servers.
  • Pairing credentials (for example Samsung tokens and LG webOS client keys) are stored in the device Keychain and do not leave the device.

B. Personal identity data

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, precise location, photos, or files. If you email us for support, we only see the information you choose to send in that message.

C. Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions and lifetime unlocks are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple may provide the App with anonymous purchase entitlements (for example, whether a subscription is active). We do not store or process your credit card, Apple ID password, or billing address.

3. Permissions

Local Network access is required to scan for and communicate with TVs on the same Wi‑Fi network. This permission is used only for TV discovery and remote-control traffic on your local network. The App does not use Local Network access to profile other people or devices for advertising.

4. How information is used

On-device information is used only to:

  • Discover compatible TVs and keep a device list you can select;
  • Maintain an authorized connection, including stored pairing keys;
  • Unlock Premium features you purchased through Apple;
  • Remember in-app preferences such as haptics.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not share TV addresses or pairing credentials with third parties. Apple processes App Store payments and related receipts under Apple’s own policies. If a future version adds optional cloud features, we will update this policy before collecting additional data.

6. Retention

Local device lists, preferences, and Keychain items remain on your iPhone until you delete them in the App or uninstall the App. Uninstalling the App removes locally stored App data, subject to iOS behavior.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us by email, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your choices and rights

Because core features work without an account, there is no cloud profile to export. You can:

  • Revoke Local Network access in iOS Settings;
  • Delete saved TVs or uninstall the App;
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID subscription settings;
  • Email us to ask how the App handles data or to request deletion of any information you sent by email.

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK, or California), you may have additional rights regarding personal data. Contact us and we will respond as required by applicable law. Because we do not run a user database for the App, many requests will relate only to support emails you sent us.

9. International use

The App processes local-network data on your device. Support emails you send to us may be received in the country where we read that mailbox. Do not include pairing tokens, passwords, or unnecessary network details in email.

10. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The “Last updated” date will change, and the current version will remain published at:

https://asynch1889.github.io/tv-remote-legal/privacy/

Material changes will be reflected on this page. Continued use of the App after an update means you acknowledge the revised policy, except where mandatory law requires additional consent.

2. 在你设备上处理的信息

A. 局域网数据

为了发现并控制智能电视（例如 Roku、Samsung，以及实验性的 LG 支持），本应用使用 Apple 网络接口，在同一 Wi‑Fi 网络上进行 SSDP、Bonjour/mDNS 等本地发现。

  • 已发现电视的本地 IP 地址、设备名称和连接端点可能保存在你的 iPhone 上，便于再次连接。
  • 我们不会把本地 IP 地址、Wi‑Fi 名称或家庭网络拓扑上传到我们的服务器。
  • 配对凭据（例如 Samsung token、LG webOS client key）仅保存在设备钥匙串（Keychain）中，不会离开本机。

B. 身份类个人信息

我们不收集你的姓名、电子邮箱、电话、通讯录、精确位置、照片或文件。如果你通过邮件联系支持，我们只会看到你在该邮件中主动提供的内容。

C. 购买与订阅

订阅和终身解锁通过 Apple StoreKit 处理。Apple 可能向本应用提供匿名的购买权益状态（例如订阅是否有效）。我们不存储或处理你的信用卡、Apple ID 密码或账单地址。

3. 权限

本地网络权限仅用于扫描并与同一 Wi‑Fi 上的电视通信，用于电视发现和遥控流量。本应用不会把本地网络权限用于广告画像。

4. 信息如何使用

设备本地信息仅用于：

  • 发现兼容电视并维护可供选择的设备列表；
  • 维持已授权连接，包括保存配对密钥；
  • 解锁你通过 Apple 购买的 Premium 功能；
  • 记住应用内偏好（例如触觉反馈）。

5. 共享

我们不出售个人信息，也不会向第三方共享电视地址或配对凭据。Apple 按照其自身政策处理 App Store 付款与收据。如果未来版本增加可选云端功能，我们会在收集额外数据前更新本政策。
