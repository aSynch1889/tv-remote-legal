Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how TV Remote Univ (“we”, “us”, or “the App”) handles user data when you use our native iOS application.

生效日期：2026 年 8 月 10 日 · 最近更新：2026 年 11 月 24 日

隐私政策

本隐私政策说明 TV Remote Univ（“我们”或“本应用”）在你使用这款原生 iOS 应用时如何处理信息。

1. Summary

  • The App is a local Wi‑Fi / LAN remote that discovers and controls compatible smart TVs on your home network.
  • No account, registration, or user profile is required.
  • We do not upload your local TV IP addresses, home network topology, pairing keys, or cast media to developer-operated servers.
  • Payments are processed by Apple via StoreKit, and anonymous purchase receipt / entitlement validation is handled through RevenueCat strictly for app functionality.
  • We do not integrate advertising networks, data brokers, or cross-app tracking SDKs (NSPrivacyTracking = false).

2. Information we collect and process

A. Local network data

To discover and connect to compatible smart TVs (such as Roku, Samsung, and experimental LG support) on your home network, the App uses Apple’s Network.framework to perform local multicast discovery (SSDP and Bonjour/mDNS).

  • Local IP addresses, device names, and connection endpoints of discovered or manually added TVs are stored locally on your iPhone for connection management and quick reconnection.
  • We do not transmit, upload, or collect your local IP addresses, Wi‑Fi SSID, or network topology on any external server.
  • Authentication credentials (such as Samsung pairing tokens and LG webOS client keys) are stored solely in your device’s secure Keychain, never leave the device, and are erased when you choose Forget / Forget free TV in the App.

B. Personal & identity data

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or precise location. If you contact support by email, we only receive the information you voluntarily include in your message.

C. Purchase & entitlement data

  • Payments for the one-time Lifetime in-app purchase are processed directly by Apple through StoreKit. We never receive, store, or process your credit card, Apple ID password, or billing address.
  • To verify and restore your Lifetime purchase entitlement across sessions, the App uses RevenueCat (Purchases SDK), which processes non-identifying App Store transaction receipts / purchase history and a randomly generated anonymous App User ID solely for app functionality (entitlement verification and purchase restoration). This purchase history data is not linked to your personal identity and is never used for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

D. Cast photos & videos

When you choose a photo or video to cast, the selected album item is copied (and converted on-device to a TV-compatible JPEG format when needed) into temporary App sandbox storage and served over a short-lived, tokenized HTTP URL on your local network so the selected compatible TV can play it. Selected media is never uploaded to external servers. Anyone on the same local network who obtains the complete temporary URL while the session is active could request that file, so do not cast sensitive media on an untrusted network. The local HTTP server stops and temporary files are removed when casting stops, the TV changes, the App moves to the background, or the session expires.

3. Permissions required

  • Local Network (NSLocalNetworkUsageDescription): Required strictly to discover, control, and stream user-selected cast media to compatible smart TVs on the same local network.
  • System Photos Picker: Used only when you choose a photo or video to cast. The App uses Apple’s out-of-process picker, does not request broad photo-library read permissions, and only accesses the specific file you select.

4. Third-party services & no-tracking commitment

  • Apple App Store / StoreKit: Processes payments and signed transactions under Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat: Used strictly as an in-app purchase receipt validation and entitlement processor (https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/).
  • No Advertising or Tracking SDKs: We do not sell data or track you across third-party apps or websites.

5. Retention & your choices

Saved TV entries, in-app preferences (theme, language, haptics), and Keychain pairing keys stay on your device until you forget the device in the App or uninstall the App. Temporary cast files are deleted automatically when casting ends or expires. You can revoke Local Network permission at any time in iOS Settings, forget saved TVs inside the App, or email us to request deletion of any support correspondence you sent.

6. Children

The App is rated 4+ as a general utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child sent personal information to our support email, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

1. 摘要

  • 本应用是家庭局域网智能电视遥控器，用于在同一网络下发现并控制兼容电视。
  • 无需注册、登录或创建个人账号。
  • 我们不会把你的本地电视 IP 地址、家庭网络拓扑、配对密钥或投屏媒体上传到开发者服务器。
  • 应用内买断付款由 Apple StoreKit 直接处理；匿名收据与买断权益校验通过 RevenueCat 完成，仅用于应用核心功能。
  • 本应用不集成任何广告联盟、数据经纪商或跨应用追踪 SDK（NSPrivacyTracking = false）。

2. 我们收集与处理的信息

A. 局域网数据

为了在家庭网络中发现并连接兼容智能电视（如 Roku、Samsung 及实验性支持的 LG 机型），本应用使用 Apple 原生 Network.framework 执行本地组播搜索（SSDP 与 Bonjour/mDNS）。

  • 已发现或手动添加电视的本地 IP 地址、设备名称与连接端口仅保存在你的 iPhone 本地，用于连接管理与快速重连。
  • 我们绝不会向任何外部服务器传输、上传或收集你的本地 IP 地址、Wi‑Fi 名称或家庭网络结构。
  • 配对鉴权凭据（如 Samsung 配对令牌与 LG webOS 客户端密钥）仅加密存储在设备系统钥匙串（Keychain）中，绝不离开本机，并在你在应用内执行忘记设备时同步擦除。

B. 个人身份数据

我们不收集你的姓名、邮箱、手机号、通讯录或精确地理位置。仅当你主动向支持邮箱发送邮件时，我们才会看到你在邮件中提供的信息。

C. 购买与权益数据

  • 一次性终身买断（Lifetime）内购付款由 Apple 通过 StoreKit 直接处理，我们不会接收、存储或处理你的银行卡、Apple ID 密码或账单地址。
  • 为在会话间校验和恢复你的终身买断权益，本应用使用 RevenueCat（Purchases SDK）处理非识别性的 App Store 交易收据/购买历史及随机生成的匿名 App User ID，仅用于应用功能（权益校验与恢复购买）。该数据不会关联你的个人身份，也绝不会用于第三方广告或跨应用追踪。

D. 照片与视频投屏

当你主动选择相册照片或视频进行投屏时，所选媒体会在本机复制到应用临时沙盒目录（必要时在本机转换为电视兼容的 JPEG 格式），并通过带一次性随机令牌的短时局域网 HTTP 链接供所选兼容电视读取播放，不会上传到任何外部服务器。在投屏会话有效期间，同一局域网内若其他设备获知完整临时链接且可访问你的 iPhone，理论上可请求该文件，因此请勿在不受信任的公共网络中投屏敏感内容。停止投屏、切换电视、应用切入后台或会话超时后，本地 HTTP 服务会立即关闭并清理临时文件。

3. 所需系统权限

  • 本地网络（NSLocalNetworkUsageDescription）：仅用于在同一家庭网络中扫描、控制兼容电视及传输你主动选择的投屏媒体。
  • 系统相册选择器（PhotosPicker）：仅在你选择要投屏的照片或视频时调用系统进程外选择器，不申请完整相册读取权限，仅读取你当次选中的单个文件。

4. 第三方服务与反追踪承诺

  • Apple App Store / StoreKit：按 Apple 隐私政策处理付款与交易签名。
  • RevenueCat：仅作为应用内购买收据校验与买断权益处理器（详见 https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy/）。
  • 无广告与无追踪 SDK：我们不出售任何数据，也不进行跨应用或跨网站追踪。

5. 数据留存与你的权利

本地保存的电视列表、偏好设置（主题、语言、触觉反馈）及钥匙串配对凭据保留在你的 iPhone 上，直至你在应用内忘记设备或卸载应用。临时投屏文件在会话结束或过期后自动清理。你可随时在 iOS“设置”中关闭本地网络权限、在应用内删除已保存电视，或发送邮件要求删除你曾发送的支持邮件记录。

6. 未成年人保护

本应用为 4+ 通用工具类应用，不会故意收集儿童个人信息。如发现儿童通过支持邮件发送了个人信息，请联系我们立即删除。
