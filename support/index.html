Before requesting help

  1. Confirm your iPhone and TV are connected to the same home Wi‑Fi network (disable router guest-network / AP client isolation).
  2. In iOS Settings → Apps → TV Remote Univ, make sure Local Network access is enabled.
  3. Make sure network / IP remote control (such as Roku “Control by mobile apps” or LG “LG Connect Apps”) is enabled on your television.
  4. For Samsung or LG TVs, watch the TV screen during first connection and approve the pairing prompt. If a TV previously revoked access, swipe left on the TV in the device picker to Forget it and reconnect.
  5. If multicast discovery is blocked by your router, tap + (Add by IP) in the TV selector and enter your TV’s local IP address directly.

Supported scope

  • Roku: Primary compatibility target (discovery, remote keys, one-tap app launch, and photo/video casting via Roku Media Player).
  • Samsung (Tizen): Remote control over local WebSocket (requires on-TV approval; capabilities vary by Tizen year/firmware).
  • LG (webOS): Experimental support (varies by webOS version, pointer socket availability, and pairing settings).
  • Not advertised as full remote targets: Sony, Vizio, Android TV, Google Cast, and AirPlay devices.

Purchases & device slots

  • The current release offers Premium solely as a one-time Lifetime purchase. It does not auto-renew and has no subscription to cancel.
  • Free tier supports 1 physical TV (plus the built-in demo Simulator). To replace your free TV, swipe to Forget the old TV in the selector or tap Forget free TV in Settings.
  • After switching or restoring an iPhone, tap Restore Purchases in Settings or on the paywall using the same Apple Account.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at https://reportaproblem.apple.com/.

Contact & bug reports

Email 9366321@gmail.com and include:

  • App version and build number;
  • iPhone model and iOS version;
  • TV brand, model, year, and firmware version;
  • Whether discovery, pairing approval, casting, or a specific key failed.
For your security, do not send pairing tokens, client keys, TV serial numbers, or public IP addresses.

联系支持前请先排查

  1. 确认 iPhone 与电视连接在同一个家庭 Wi‑Fi 下（请勿连接开启了 AP 客户端隔离的访客网络，连接时请暂关全局代理 VPN）。
  2. 前往 iOS 设置 → App → 电视遥控器（TV Remote Univ），确认已开启本地网络权限。
  3. 确认电视设置中已允许局域网/移动应用控制（如 Roku 的“Control by mobile apps”或 LG 的“LG Connect Apps”）。
  4. 首次连接 Samsung 或 LG 电视时，请留意电视屏幕弹出的授权提示并点击允许；若电视曾撤销授权，可在选择电视列表左滑忘记该电视后重新连接。
  5. 若路由器屏蔽了局域网组播导致自动扫描不到电视，可点击电视选择器右上角的 +（按 IP 添加） 直接输入电视局域网 IP 连接。

支持范围说明

  • Roku：主要兼容目标（支持局域网发现、基础与进阶按键、一键应用启动及照片/视频投屏）。
  • Samsung（Tizen）：支持局域网 WebSocket 遥控（首次需在电视端允许连接，按键能力因机型与固件而异）。
  • LG（webOS）：实验性支持（取决于 webOS 固件版本、指针服务可用性与配对设置）。
  • 不作为完整遥控承诺对象：Sony、Vizio、Android TV、Google Cast 与 AirPlay 设备。

购买与免费设备槽位

  • 当前版本高级版（Premium）仅提供一次性终身买断，不会自动续订，不存在需要取消的周期性扣费。
  • 免费版支持绑定 1 台真实电视（内置演示模拟器不占名额）；如需更换免费电视，可在设备列表中左滑忘记旧电视，或在设置页点击忘记免费电视释放槽位。
  • 更换或重置 iPhone 后，使用同一 Apple 账户在设置页或付费页点击恢复购买即可找回终身高级版权益。
  • 退款申请由 Apple 官方统一受理：https://reportaproblem.apple.com/。
